Location Decision Support for Small Business Planning

This dashboard helps users evaluate potential store locations in Worcester, MA using a Huff Model. The system estimates how much customer demand a proposed location may capture based on business type, store size, geographic location, and nearby competitors in the same NAICS market.

Users can enter a store type or NAICS code, select a proposed location on the map or type latitude and longitude, and enter the planned floor area. The dashboard then returns predicted visits, estimated market share, nearby competitors' current market share, and a plain-English explanation to support location decision-making.

Important comparison rule: saved scenarios should only be compared when they use the same NAICS code / business category. A department store scenario should not be compared with a cafe scenario.

Inputs Store type, NAICS code, location, and floor area
Outputs Predicted visits, proposed-store market share, and competitors' current market share
